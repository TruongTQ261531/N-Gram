# Execution history and corrections

29 October 2026. The purpose of this file is to keep development, confirmation, diagnostics and execution amendments distinguishable. A passed CI check is not a publication verdict.

1. **Prior studies preserved.** CAPC and BOLT remain research archives with their original negative/limited conclusions. This round changes scope to inference-time retrieval certification, not an assumed effective CAPC–BOLT hybrid. Their existing scientific artifacts are not rewritten.
2. **Strict-set pilot failed.** Twenty SciFact training and twenty NFCorpus development queries gave zero strict top-10 set-invariance certificates for nonzero budgets. Source, protocol, outputs and logs remain in `audit/set_invariance_screen_v0/`. The weaker winner-retention property was selected on development before test metrics, with the original pilot protocol kept alongside the active retention protocol.
3. **Development implementation.** Added shared-query affine count bounds, a same-formulation generic LP control, safe corpus-level screening, discrete incompatibility/cover, feasible exhaustive enumeration and generic MILP. Ordinary transpose caching fixed a scipy CSR/CSC conversion cost and applies equally to controls. `audit/pre_transpose_cache_core.py` preserves the earlier version; it is not the frozen predictor.
4. **Routine errors corrected before confirmation.** A metadata script used an unavailable `requests` import and was changed to urllib; a sparse `len()` misuse was caught before output; a sampled-K assertion and timing accounting omission were corrected before the final source freeze. These are implementation corrections, not discarded test failures or improved held-out outcomes. Existing smoke/development logs and outputs are retained with their own labels.
5. **Arithmetic and reference tests.** Eighteen tests passed before confirmation, including exhaustive random comparisons, LP/MILP references, discrete incompatibility and Decimal intervals. The randomized LP loop generates 60 cases, of which 49 meet its negative-lower-margin precondition; the others are skipped, not claimed solved. Counts appear in `results/validation_counts.json`.
6. **Primary prospective freeze.** Commit `8dd7972dc13d85159e908f9b6ea03c394289acd2` published source/configuration, three 100-query selections, input/index audits and 17-file freeze before primary test metrics. `results/execution_events.jsonl` records starts/finishes. All three primary workers exited zero and all 300 selected queries were retained.
7. **Primary findings preserved.** B2/K10 gains over best LP+enumeration were 5/4/5 points but the original exact paired/Holm-18 evidence was insufficient for a general superiority claim. All B/K/method results, including MILP wins, remain in the release. Target selection for another stage does not retroactively change the first protocol.
8. **Thread execution deviation disclosed.** The original driver imported numpy before environment setup, initializing OpenBLAS with three threads rather than the protocol's one; HiGHS was explicitly one. This was found after primary completion. No source/score was tuned in response. `audit/threading_observation.json` records the issue; concurrent primary timings are not used for speed claims. Controlled replay initializes environment before imports and uses threadpool limits, sequential fresh corpus processes and independent VmHWM readings.
9. **Full primary replay.** All 300 complete rank profiles and all 300 B2-restricted profiles agree. Full-corpus re-encoding reconstructs all 27,000 seeded sampled predictions and saved support/MIP witnesses. NPZ exports permit independent metric reconstruction; 12 real-query margin fixtures and three generic synthetic gap cases are additionally audited. Eight real fixtures are independently enumerated. These are validation/diagnostics, not new untouched samples.
10. **Independent second-stage preregistration.** After the primary results, B2/K10 versus best LP+enumeration was chosen. Commit `5fcf816da7d686427f518845c71e682d95626cee` published the separate protocol, fixed 970 fresh inputs (199/223/548), 23-file freeze and the original result hashes before fresh metrics. Input IDs and effective 32-token text are disjoint from every evaluated development/primary input; the new inputs are deduplicated. All eligible inputs are used with no sequential significance stopping. No stage pooling is used to rescue primary p-values.
11. **Execution-only FiQA partition amendment.** SciFact/NFCorpus exited zero. The interruption is not hidden as a successful original run.
